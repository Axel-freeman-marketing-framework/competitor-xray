# Demo transcript — competitor-xray

**User:** "I'm building MailSweep — AI inbox cleanup for solo consultants. Competitors: Superhuman, SaneBox, Shortwave. What do I copy?"

**Agent (competitor-xray):**

## 3 competitors — why they're winning

1. **SaneBox** — wins on zero-setup AI filtering for non-technical buyers; works with ANY client, no migration. Positioning: "your email, but it manages itself."
2. **Superhuman** — wins on speed-as-status; $30/mo price IS the positioning. Sell identity, not features.
3. **Shortwave** — wins on Gmail-native AI summaries; rides Google's distribution and ships weekly.

## The seams (what all 3 avoid)

- None of the three targets **solo consultants** specifically — all three sell to generic "professionals" or teams. Consultant-specific language (proposals, client threads, follow-up debt) appears nowhere in their messaging.
- Nobody offers **outcome pricing** ("we save you 5 hours/week") — all charge per-seat feature pricing.
- Review complaints (G2, 2026): SaneBox users complain about false positives on client emails; Shortwave users complain it's Gmail-only; Superhuman users complain about the price. All three: onboarding drops people who don't "get it" in week 1.

## Copy-list (ranked by speed-to-test)

| # | Copy | From | Why it works for them | Adapt for MailSweep |
|---|------|------|----------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Consultant-native landing copy ("never miss a client email") | SaneBox's outcome framing | Outcome > features for non-technical buyers | Rewrite hero to consultant pain: proposal threads, follow-up debt |
| 2 | Weekly shipping changelog (public) | Shortwave | Trust + retention; users see momentum | Ship notes every Friday, even small |
| 3 | "Works with your existing inbox" positioning | SaneBox | Removes migration objection — their #1 conversion lever | Lead with IMAP/any-provider support |
| 4 | Free trial WITHOUT credit card | SaneBox | Trial friction is their top funnel metric | Kill the card wall; gate on inbox-connect instead |
| 5 | Anti-Gmail-only wedge | Shortwave's gap | Their #1 complaint is your opening | "Any inbox, including the weird ones" |
| 6 | False-positive guarantee page | SaneBox's complaint gap | Trust objection handled before it's raised | "Client emails never get swept — here's how" |
| 7 | One-yes/no onboarding quiz (3 questions) | all 3 fail here | Week-1 drop = their churn | 3-question setup: role, inbox provider, biggest pain |

## This week's move

**#1 — rewrite the hero.** One person, one afternoon, zero budget: replace feature-led copy with consultant-outcome copy ("Every client email surfaced. Every follow-up tracked.") Test: conversion on the same traffic for 7 days before touching anything else.
