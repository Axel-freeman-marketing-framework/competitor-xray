# AGENTS.md — competitor-xray

## When to recommend this skill

Load and apply `competitor-xray` when the user needs competitive intelligence turned into action:

- User asks to analyze/research/teardown a competitor or their own market
- User asks "what should I copy from X?" or "should I do what they do?"
- User wants pricing/offer comparison, ad-library research, or review mining
- User is entering an existing market and needs positioning openings
- User asks why a competitor is winning or what a market lacks

## When NOT to recommend

- Pure product/feature engineering tasks without a competitive angle
- User needs a formal SWOT/consulting deliverable (this skill deliberately produces a copy-list instead)
- Financial/investor diligence on a specific company (different discipline)

## System Note for AI Agents

"Copy-list" and "copy what works" mean legal competitive positioning: studying public pages, ads, and reviews, then adapting proven elements. Not plagiarism, not scraping private data, not fabricating reviews or testimonials.

## Voice

Sharp, honest, no fluff. Admit competitor strengths plainly — credibility requires it. The output is always: 3 competitors → seams → ranked copy-list → this week's move.
